# When to ask

## Scope

When to work on your own and when to stop and ask whoever owns the decision, such as the user, a maintainer, or a collaborator. Applies to anyone, and matters most when working without supervision. The aim is to work autonomously without going off the rails, with code that stays a means to an end and remains maintainable as the project grows.

## Guidelines

### Proceed on your own inside the agreed scope

When the work stays within the request, the plan, the design, and the [risk tier](risk-tiers.md), decide and continue without asking. Asking about settled matters slows the work and trains people to ignore questions.

### Stop and ask when the work is bigger or different than agreed

Stop and ask when any of these happens:

- The work touches more than the plan listed, or will take much longer than expected.
- The work moves up a risk tier, or to a larger kind of approach.
- A finding contradicts the request, the plan, or the design, or the design does not hold up in practice.
- The next step is irreversible, High risk, or reaches shared systems, and was not approved.
- A safeguard blocks the work, or the instructions conflict and the right choice is unclear ([precedence](precedence.md)).

Do not absorb these silently. A quiet workaround leaves the owner with a different result than they agreed to.

### Decide small things and say so

When a question has a low-cost answer that is easy to undo, choose, and state the choice and the reason where the owner will see it. Ask about decisions that are costly to reverse, or that change what the owner gets.

### Ask with a short summary

Say what was expected, what you found, the options with their costs, and the option you recommend. Make the question answerable without rereading the whole work. A vague "this is harder than expected" forces the owner to rediscover the problem.

### Keep working only on what stays valid

While waiting, continue only work that is correct under every option. Do not build on a guess, and do not work around the blocker. Say what is paused.

### Record the answer

Put the answer where the plan and [decision records](decision-records.md) live, and update the plan. Later work and later readers then follow the decision and not the earlier assumption.

## Exceptions

If the owner has told you to proceed regardless of a trigger, do so and state which trigger you passed. A trigger that puts others at risk, such as exposing data, still gets a question.
