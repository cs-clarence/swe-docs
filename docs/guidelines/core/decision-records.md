# Decision records

## Scope

When and how to record a significant decision so later readers know why. Excludes routine choices.

## Guidelines

### Record decisions that are costly to reverse

Write a record when a decision affects several components, a contract, or data, or is hard to undo. Skip choices a later reader can see are arbitrary.

### Capture context, options, and reason

State the problem, the options considered, the choice, and why. The reason is what code cannot show.

### Record the consequences and revisit conditions

Say what becomes harder, and what would make the decision worth revisiting.

### Keep records next to the work and do not edit history

Store records where the project keeps its documentation. Supersede an outdated record with a new one that links to it, so the history stays readable.

## Exceptions

Small projects may record decisions in a single list; keep the same content.
