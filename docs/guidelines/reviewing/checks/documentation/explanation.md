# Checks: explanation

Verifies [authoring/documentation/explanation](../../../authoring/documentation/explanation.md). Tier = lowest [risk tier](../../../core/risk-tiers.md) at which to check.

| Check | Rule | Tier |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Does it answer why, including alternatives not chosen? | [answer why](../../../authoring/documentation/explanation.md#answer-why) | Low |
| Is it bounded to one topic? | [give each document one topic](../../../authoring/documentation/explanation.md#give-each-document-one-topic) | Low |
| Are trade-offs and limits stated? | [state trade-offs and limits](../../../authoring/documentation/explanation.md#state-trade-offs-and-limits) | Low |
| Is it free of steps and option tables? | [do not give steps or tables of options](../../../authoring/documentation/explanation.md#do-not-give-steps-or-tables-of-options) | Low |
| Are proposals and decisions linked, not mixed in? | [keep it separate from decisions in progress](../../../authoring/documentation/explanation.md#keep-it-separate-from-decisions-in-progress) | Low |

## Blocking

All optional.
