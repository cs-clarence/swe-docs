# Checks: tutorials

Verifies [authoring/documentation/tutorials](../../../authoring/documentation/tutorials.md). Tier = lowest [risk tier](../../../core/risk-tiers.md) at which to check.

| Check | Rule | Tier |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Does it lead to one concrete outcome? | [lead to one concrete outcome](../../../authoring/documentation/tutorials.md#lead-to-one-concrete-outcome) | Low |
| Are prerequisites and the starting state stated? | [state the starting point](../../../authoring/documentation/tutorials.md#state-the-starting-point) | Low |
| Does every step succeed from a clean start? | [make every step succeed](../../../authoring/documentation/tutorials.md#make-every-step-succeed) | Medium |
| Does each step say what the reader should see? | [show results as you go](../../../authoring/documentation/tutorials.md#show-results-as-you-go) | Low |
| Does it avoid explanations and branches? | [do not explain or branch](../../../authoring/documentation/tutorials.md#do-not-explain-or-branch) | Low |

## Blocking

Blocking: steps that do not work as written. Others are optional.
