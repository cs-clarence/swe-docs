# Checks: how-to-guides

Verifies [authoring/documentation/how-to-guides](../../../authoring/documentation/how-to-guides.md). Tier = lowest [risk tier](../../../core/risk-tiers.md) at which to check.

| Check | Rule | Tier |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Is it titled by the reader's goal? | [name the task as the reader's goal](../../../authoring/documentation/how-to-guides.md#name-the-task-as-the-readers-goal) | Low |
| Does it start from the task, not from teaching basics? | [assume the reader knows the basics](../../../authoring/documentation/how-to-guides.md#assume-the-reader-knows-the-basics) | Low |
| Are steps ordered, one action each, with a way to check success? | [give ordered steps with one action each](../../../authoring/documentation/how-to-guides.md#give-ordered-steps-with-one-action-each) | Medium |
| Does it keep to the task and link for options? | [cover the task, not every option](../../../authoring/documentation/how-to-guides.md#cover-the-task-not-every-option) | Low |
| Are likely failures covered at their step? | [cover the likely failures](../../../authoring/documentation/how-to-guides.md#cover-the-likely-failures) | Medium |

## Blocking

Blocking: steps that do not work as written. Others are optional.
