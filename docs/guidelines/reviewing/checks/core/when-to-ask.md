# Checks: when to ask

Verifies [core/when-to-ask](../../../core/when-to-ask.md). Tier = lowest [risk tier](../../../core/risk-tiers.md) at which to check.

| Check | Rule | Tier |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Did the work continue without needless questions on settled matters? | [proceed on your own inside the agreed scope](../../../core/when-to-ask.md#proceed-on-your-own-inside-the-agreed-scope) | Low |
| Where the work grew, changed tier, contradicted the plan, or needed an unapproved risky action, did it stop and ask? | [stop and ask when the work is bigger or different than agreed](../../../core/when-to-ask.md#stop-and-ask-when-the-work-is-bigger-or-different-than-agreed) | Medium |
| Were small, reversible choices made and stated, and costly ones put to the owner? | [decide small things and say so](../../../core/when-to-ask.md#decide-small-things-and-say-so) | Low |
| Does the question say what was expected, what was found, the options, and a recommendation? | [ask with a short summary](../../../core/when-to-ask.md#ask-with-a-short-summary) | Medium |
| While waiting, was only valid-under-every-option work continued, and the pause stated? | [keep working only on what stays valid](../../../core/when-to-ask.md#keep-working-only-on-what-stays-valid) | Medium |
| Is the user's answer recorded and the plan updated? | [record the answer](../../../core/when-to-ask.md#record-the-answer) | Medium |

## Blocking

At High risk, work that continued past a trigger without asking goes back to the author. Others are should fix. Finding classes are in [process](../../process.md#classify-each-finding).
