# Checks: new project

Verifies [planning/approaches/new-project](../../../../planning/approaches/new-project.md). Tier = lowest [risk tier](../../../../core/risk-tiers.md) at which to check.

| Check | Rule | Tier |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Are the purpose, users, and success criteria stated? | [settle purpose, users, and success before technology](../../../../planning/approaches/new-project.md#settle-purpose-users-and-success-before-technology) | Medium |
| Were the riskiest assumptions tested, with prototypes labeled or discarded? | [test the riskiest assumptions with a prototype](../../../../planning/approaches/new-project.md#test-the-riskiest-assumptions-with-a-prototype) | High |
| Are tool choices proven, or justified and recorded? | [choose proven tools unless a need says otherwise](../../../../planning/approaches/new-project.md#choose-proven-tools-unless-a-need-says-otherwise) | Medium |
| Is there a worked example of the happy path, complexities, and edge cases that the work is built to satisfy? | [define an example that exercises the hard parts](../../../../planning/approaches/new-project.md#define-an-example-that-exercises-the-hard-parts) | Medium |
| Does a thin end-to-end slice exist, with build, test, release, and monitoring? | [build a thin end-to-end slice first](../../../../planning/approaches/new-project.md#build-a-thin-end-to-end-slice-first) | Medium |
| Are automated checks and a repeatable build in place before features? | [set up verification before the features](../../../../planning/approaches/new-project.md#set-up-verification-before-the-features) | Medium |
| Are the costly-to-change decisions made and recorded early? | [decide early what is costly to change later](../../../../planning/approaches/new-project.md#decide-early-what-is-costly-to-change-later) | High |
| Is the structure no more complex than current needs require? | [keep the structure as simple as the need](../../../../planning/approaches/new-project.md#keep-the-structure-as-simple-as-the-need) | Medium |
| Is the first version small, with its scope and exclusions stated? | [define a small first version](../../../../planning/approaches/new-project.md#define-a-small-first-version) | Medium |
| Are conventions written down? | [write the conventions down early](../../../../planning/approaches/new-project.md#write-the-conventions-down-early) | Low |

## Blocking

At High risk, work planned under a smaller kind than it needs, or without the plan its kind requires, goes back to the author. Others are should fix. Finding classes are in [process](../../../process.md#classify-each-finding).
