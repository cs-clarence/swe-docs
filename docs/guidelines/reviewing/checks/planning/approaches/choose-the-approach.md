# Checks: choose the approach

Verifies [planning/approaches/choose-the-approach](../../../../planning/approaches/choose-the-approach.md). Tier = lowest [risk tier](../../../../core/risk-tiers.md) at which to check.

| Check | Rule | Tier |
| --- | --- | --- |
| How much changes, how much is unknown, how hard it is to undo, and how many people it needs? | [assess the work on four questions](../../../../planning/approaches/choose-the-approach.md#assess-the-work-on-four-questions) | Medium |
| Is rigor taken from the risk tier and not from the size? | [take rigor from the risk tier, not the size](../../../../planning/approaches/choose-the-approach.md#take-rigor-from-the-risk-tier-not-the-size) | Medium |
| Is the chosen kind the lightest that covers the work? | [pick the lightest approach that fits](../../../../planning/approaches/choose-the-approach.md#pick-the-lightest-approach-that-fits) | Low |
| If unknowns dominate, was a bounded experiment run before the kind was chosen? | [resolve unknowns before choosing](../../../../planning/approaches/choose-the-approach.md#resolve-unknowns-before-choosing) | Medium |
| Are the chosen kind and the reason stated? | [state the chosen kind and why](../../../../planning/approaches/choose-the-approach.md#state-the-chosen-kind-and-why) | Medium |
| Was the kind reassessed when the work outgrew the plan? | [reassess as the work grows](../../../../planning/approaches/choose-the-approach.md#reassess-as-the-work-grows) | Medium |

## Blocking

At High risk, work planned under a smaller kind than it needs, or without the plan its kind requires, goes back to the author. Others are should fix. Finding classes are in [process](../../../process.md#classify-each-finding).
