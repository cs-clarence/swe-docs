# Checks: large work

Verifies [planning/approaches/large-work](../../../../planning/approaches/large-work.md). Tier = lowest [risk tier](../../../../core/risk-tiers.md) at which to check.

| Check | Rule | Tier |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Are the plan, end state, and decisions recorded before work starts? | [write the plan and record the decisions first](../../../../planning/approaches/large-work.md#write-the-plan-and-record-the-decisions-first) | High |
| Is there a worked example of the happy path, complexities, and edge cases that the work is built to satisfy? | [define an example that exercises the hard parts](../../../../planning/approaches/large-work.md#define-an-example-that-exercises-the-hard-parts) | High |
| Was the riskiest assumption tested before the bulk of the work? | [test the riskiest assumption first](../../../../planning/approaches/large-work.md#test-the-riskiest-assumption-first) | High |
| Is the work cut into end-to-end slices that can ship? | [deliver in thin end-to-end slices](../../../../planning/approaches/large-work.md#deliver-in-thin-end-to-end-slices) | High |
| Can each stage be released gradually and reversed? | [release in stages that can be reversed](../../../../planning/approaches/large-work.md#release-in-stages-that-can-be-reversed) | High |
| Are ownership and the interfaces between parts agreed? | [agree ownership and interfaces up front](../../../../planning/approaches/large-work.md#agree-ownership-and-interfaces-up-front) | High |

## Blocking

At High risk, work planned under a smaller kind than it needs, or without the plan its kind requires, goes back to the author. Others are should fix. Finding classes are in [process](../../../process.md#classify-each-finding).
