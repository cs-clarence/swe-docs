# Checks: medium work

Verifies [planning/approaches/medium-work](../../../../planning/approaches/medium-work.md). Tier = lowest [risk tier](../../../../core/risk-tiers.md) at which to check.

| Check | Rule | Tier |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Was the affected area read and its behavior confirmed? | [understand the affected area before choosing an approach](../../../../planning/approaches/medium-work.md#understand-the-affected-area-before-choosing-an-approach) | Medium |
| Is there a short written plan with goal, steps, risks, and verification? | [write a short plan first](../../../../planning/approaches/medium-work.md#write-a-short-plan-first) | Medium |
| Were the interfaces between parts decided first? | [settle the interfaces before the internals](../../../../planning/approaches/medium-work.md#settle-the-interfaces-before-the-internals) | Medium |
| Is the work split into ordered, reviewable steps? | [break it into two to five reviewable steps](../../../../planning/approaches/medium-work.md#break-it-into-two-to-five-reviewable-steps) | Medium |
| Was the work moved up a kind when it outgrew its kind? | [move up a kind when it grows](../../../../planning/approaches/medium-work.md#move-up-a-kind-when-it-grows) | Medium |

## Blocking

At High risk, work planned under a smaller kind than it needs, or without the plan its kind requires, goes back to the author. Others are should fix. Finding classes are in [process](../../../process.md#classify-each-finding).
