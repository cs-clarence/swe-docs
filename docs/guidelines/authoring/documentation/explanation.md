# Explanation

## Scope

Documents that build understanding: context, reasons, trade-offs, and how parts fit together. For steps, see [how-to-guides](how-to-guides.md); for facts, see [reference](reference.md).

## Guidelines

### Answer why

Explain the reasons behind the design, the problem it solves, and the alternatives that were not chosen ([document the why and the limits](documentation.md#document-the-why-and-the-limits)). The reader should finish able to reason about the system.

### Give each document one topic

Bound each explanation to one idea or area. Link related explanations so none grows into a catch-all.

### State trade-offs and limits

Say what the design gives up, when it is a poor fit, and what is known to be unsettled. An explanation with no downsides is advertising.

### Do not give steps or tables of options

Link to [how-to guides](how-to-guides.md) and [reference](reference.md) when the reader needs to act or look up a fact.

### Keep it separate from decisions in progress

An explanation describes the system as it is. A proposal or an accepted choice belongs in a [design doc](../../planning/design-docs.md) or [decision record](../../core/decision-records.md); link to it.

## Exceptions

A short note of reasons may sit beside a reference entry or guide step when separating it would hurt the reader. Keep it to a few sentences and link onward.
